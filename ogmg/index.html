Less is More.

I am Gabriel Coler, an IFMGA certified mountain guide. I started guiding rock climbing at Smith Rock in 2004, and since then I have been fortunate to make my living climbing and skiing with some of my best friends.

Old Growth Mountain Guides was created to give the best experience to all of those friends I have met over the years. I am also excited to meet new people and share our local crag of Smith Rock, or the deep powder and old growth forests of the Cascades.
