Old Growth Mountain Guides: logo concepts

Four directions, each in a square and a horizontal version (exactly 2:1). Colors come from the Cascades and Smith Rock: forest green, snow and tuff orange. The SVG files are in the logos folder.

A Tree Peak

An old-growth fir stands as the summit of the range, with a ski track carving below.

B Crag & Canopy

A Smith Rock–style tuff spire next to a towering fir, framed like a trailhead sign.

C Ring Count

Old-growth tree rings rise over the Cascades like a sun; a ski line drops down the face.

D Axe, Ski & Fir

Crossed ice axe and ski beneath a fir: climbing and skiing, rooted in the forest.
