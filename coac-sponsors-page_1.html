Have products or services to give?

We're grateful for in-kind donations for our raffles and fundraisers, and all in-kind donors are recognized in our Annual Report. Pair your donation with a cash contribution and its wholesale value can cover up to 50% of a season sponsorship level, with all the benefits of that level.

Questions, or want to contribute in a way that doesn't fit these categories? Contact us and let us know what you're thinking. We'd love to talk!
